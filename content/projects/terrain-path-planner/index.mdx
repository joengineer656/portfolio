## The question

Is the shortest path always the safest for a rover on rough ground?

## What I built

A grid planner whose cost mixes distance, slope and a loose-ground estimate, run over 200 simulated terrains.

## What happened

Tip-over risk dropped by about a third. The cost weights still need tuning on a real vehicle.
