## The question

Can one set of plates hold most of the small parts we mill?

## What I built

A grid-and-dowel plate system with soft jaws, checked in FEA for clamping deflection.

## What happened

Setup per job dropped from about 30 minutes to five. The dowel holes wear and need replacing yearly.
