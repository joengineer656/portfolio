## The question

A four-layer board can run field-oriented control at 30 A without a heatsink fight.

## What I built

Current sensing on each phase, a CAN bus for commands, and a bootloader so the firmware can be updated in the field.

## What happened

Rev B runs cool. Rev A did not, and the burn mark is still on the shelf as a reminder.
