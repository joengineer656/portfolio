## The question

Printed gearboxes can hold 0.5 mm repeatability if every joint is closed-loop.

## What I built

Six joints with an encoder on every axis. Inverse kinematics runs on the microcontroller, and ROS 2 sends it target poses.

## What happened

Hit 0.4 mm repeatability. Backlash in joint 2 is the next thing to fix.
